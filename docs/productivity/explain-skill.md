## What it does

`explain-skill` explains one skill in simple words. It reads the skill's `SKILL.md` and every file it points to, then says what it does, when to use it, what you get, and what comes before and after it, with a real example.

## When to reach for it

Type `/explain-skill <name>`, such as `/explain-skill tdd`, when you see a skill and do not know what it does.

## Prerequisites

None.
